## cow-llm-2x2

```
CHARACTER: A stout, bovine figure rendered in mixed media collage. The body is composed of textured moss-green construction paper with irregular, torn-edge white cardstock patches applied as spots. The hooves are jagged bits of dark charcoal charcoal-rubbed cardboard, and the eyes are simple white paper circles with punched-out black centers. The silhouette is blocky and slightly oversized at the chest, possessing a stiff, tactile quality that suggests layers of glue and pressed fiber.

CHOREOGRAPHY: 

The cow stands with its weight centered, front right leg lifting slightly off the ground while the head tilts downward, ears drooping forward.

The weight shifts onto the front right leg as the back left leg pushes off, causing the rear end to lift and the torso to arch gently like a piece of stiff parchment.

The back left leg plants firmly forward, causing the front left leg to retract and the head to bob upward, creating a rhythmic, rocking motion.

The weight settles back into a neutral stance, the front left leg reconnects with the ground, and the head returns to its starting position, preparing to repeat the cycle with a subtle, rhythmic bounce.
```
